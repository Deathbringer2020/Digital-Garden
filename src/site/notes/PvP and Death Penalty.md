# PvP and Death Penalty

## What happens when a player kills you

The **killer gains Combat XP** and the **victim loses Combat XP**. Both amounts are measured in "levels' worth of XP" (each player's own next level costs) and depend on the **level difference**.

| Situation (Combat levels) | Killer gains | Victim loses |
|---|---|---|
| Within ±3 levels | ≈ 3 levels | ≈ 3 levels |
| Victim 4–6 levels **higher** | ≈ 4 levels | ≈ 4 levels |
| Victim 7–9 levels **higher** | ≈ 5 levels | ≈ 5 levels |
| Victim 10+ levels **higher** | ≈ 6 levels | ≈ 6 levels |
| Killer 4–6 levels **higher** | 1 level | 1 level |
| Killer 7–9 levels **higher** | ¼ level | ¼ level |
| Killer 10+ levels **higher** | 1 ÷ (level difference) of a level | same |

So: **hunting stronger players pays off big**, while bullying weaker ones gives almost nothing. Check the **Combat Level under each name tag** before you fight.

Dying to mobs, falling, lava etc. **doesn't** cost XP.

- Every Combat level you lose also removes **1 Classes XP**. See [[Skills/Classes\|Classes]].
- Your Combat level can't go below 0.
- All other skills **never** lose levels.

## The "frozen" state (skill debt)

If you lose levels you had **already spent points on**, your Combat (or Classes) points go **negative**. When you respawn:

1. You're **frozen in place**: no walking, no jumping, and you can't be damaged.
2. After a second you also get **Blindness, Mining Fatigue and Weakness**.
3. Chat tells you: `Open /menu`.

**How to get free:**

1. Type `/menu`. Instead of the normal menu you get **"Remove Combat Upgrades"** or **"Remove Classes Upgrades"**.
2. The chest shows how many points you still need to give back.
3. Click upgrades to remove them one by one (each click refunds that upgrade's cost).
4. Once you're at 0 or more points, **close the menu**. The effects are removed, your speed comes back, and you get a short moment of damage protection.

> [!TIP]
> Avoid the freeze by keeping a few **unspent Combat points** as a buffer if you PvP a lot.

## Cheating death: Revive

With the **Revive** upgrade from the [[Skills/Enchanting Skill\|Enchanting Skill]] (25 points), your first death in a **3-hour** window is cancelled entirely. You keep your items and your XP.

## Other death rules

- Locked-slot barriers never drop on death and are restored when you respawn. See [[Skills/Inventory Skill\|Inventory Skill]].
- After a normal respawn you get a very short resistance effect.
