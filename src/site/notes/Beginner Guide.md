# Beginner Guide

Welcome! This server uses the [[Leveling Plugin\|Leveling Plugin]]. Your character starts **weak** and gets stronger by playing. Here's how to survive your first hour and make smart first choices.

## What's different when you spawn

When you join for the first time:

- You get **16 Steak**. Hold on to it.
- You have **5 hearts** (vanilla: 10).
- Only your **hotbar (9 slots)** works. The other 27 slots hold red **"Slot Locked"** barriers that you can't move.
- You **walk at half speed** and break blocks a bit slower.
- You **can't wear any armor** yet. Armor you put on pops back into your inventory.
- Your hits do **1 less damage**, and **natural healing from food is only 25%** as fast.
- You burn twice as long when on fire.

Full comparison: [[Starting Stats\|Starting Stats]].

> [!TIP]
> Take it slow in your first minutes. You're fragile, you heal slowly and you have no armor.

## Step 1: Open the menu

Type **`/menu`**. You'll see one icon per skill with your level and XP (for example `XP: 35/100`). **Left-click** an icon to open its upgrade tree. In a tree:

- The **chest** at the top shows your level and **Points Usable**. Click it to go back.
- Every other item is an upgrade. Its tooltip shows the effect and the **Cost**.
- The **iron door** (bottom right) resets the tree and refunds every point.

## Step 2: Pick your free class right away

Click the **player head (Classes)**. Your **first class costs 0 points**, so you can unlock it the second you join:

| Class | Pick it if you … |
|---|---|
| **Tank** | want more hearts and better healing |
| **Melee** | fight with swords/axes. More damage plus life steal |
| **Bare-Handed** | like punching things. Big fist damage |
| **Ranged** | use bows and crossbows |

You can unlock more classes later and level them up with Class points. See [[Skills/Classes\|Classes]].

## Step 3: Get your first levels

Everything you do anyway gives XP:

| Do this | Skill |
|---|---|
| Walk and sprint around | Movement |
| Pick up items, craft things | Inventory |
| Mine ores (not with Silk Touch) | Mining |
| Kill hostile mobs | Combat |
| Heal (eat, regenerate) | Defense |
| Harvest fully grown wheat, carrots, potatoes | Farmer |
| Fish | Fishing |
| Enchant items | Enchanting |
| Brew potions near a brewing stand | Brewing |

Exact numbers: [[Skills and Skill Points\|Skills and Skill Points]].

## Step 4: Recommended first upgrades

1. **Inventory → Unlock a slot.** The first slot is **free**, and the next few cost only 1–2 points. See [[Skills/Inventory Skill\|Inventory Skill]].
2. **Mining → Armor Level 1 (3 points).** Lets you wear leather, chainmail, copper and turtle armor. Then save for iron/gold (5 points). See [[Skills/Mining Skill\|Mining Skill]].
3. **Defense → Base Health (2 points each)** for more hearts, or **Regen Max HP** for steady healing. See [[Skills/Defense Skill\|Defense Skill]].
4. **Movement → Base Movement Speed (5 points).** Five upgrades bring you back to normal speed. See [[Skills/Movement Skill\|Movement Skill]].

The full trees are in [[Upgrade Trees\|Upgrade Trees]].

## Step 5: Know the risks

- **Dying to another player costs you Combat XP and can cost Combat levels.** The killer gains it. See [[PvP and Death Penalty\|PvP and Death Penalty]].
- If you lose levels you already spent points on, you respawn **frozen and blind** with the message `Open /menu`. Open it, remove upgrades until you're no longer in debt, close the menu and you're free.
- Other skills never lose levels. Only **Combat** (and through it **Classes**) can go down.

## Handy tips

- **Resetting is free.** Try builds and refund them if you don't like them.
- Before resetting **Inventory**, empty your extra slots. Resetting locks them again and drops what's inside.
- **Health Scaling** (Defense tree, lime terracotta) only changes how your hearts *look*.
- Mob-kill XP drops off after 60 kills in 10 minutes. Spread your grinding out.
- High-level custom potions only work for players who unlocked that level themselves. See [[Custom Potion Brewing\|Custom Potion Brewing]].
